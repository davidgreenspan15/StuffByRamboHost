Android Vision

A little help getting connected.

Your Android phone, in a floating window on Apple Vision Pro.

You need both apps. Install the matching Android Vision companion on an Android 14+ phone and the viewer on Apple Vision Pro. Keep both devices on the same Wi-Fi, and keep the phone awake and unlocked.

Start your first session

  1. Open Android Vision on both devices. On the phone, complete the phone-control setup. The current app requires this permission before starting a sharing session.
  2. On the phone, choose Pair a new device to make it discoverable, then select it from Nearby phones on the headset.
  3. Compare the eight-digit code on both devices. Approve only if all digits match.
  4. On the phone, tap Start sharing & control and approve Android’s screen-sharing prompt. The app requests the full display by default.
  5. Allow optional audio capture to hear supported media and game playback through your headset.

Do I need to approve every time?

Saved pairs reconnect without comparing a new code. Android still requires approval on the phone for every new screen-sharing session, and phone control needs your approval for that session. With the phone app open, select the saved phone on the headset. If background availability and notifications are enabled, you can also receive a sharing-request notification while another app is open; tap it and approve sharing. Android may stop background availability, in which case reopen the phone app. Pairing does not permit unattended sharing.

What happens when my phone locks?

Sharing and control stop. Unlock your phone on the phone itself, reconnect if needed, and approve a new sharing session. Remote unlocking is not supported.

I cannot find my phone

Check that both devices use the same Wi-Fi, the phone app is open, and Local Network access is allowed for Android Vision on the headset. Guest networks or network isolation can prevent devices from reaching each other. For a first pair, make the phone discoverable. Update both companion apps together.

I see a blank screen or hear no sound

Some apps block screen sharing or playback capture. Try another app to check the connection. Calls are not supported. For audio, allow the phone’s audio permission, confirm the headset is not muted, and try ordinary media playback. Microphone audio is not used. Android Vision cannot override another app’s capture restrictions.

Viewing works, but taps or typing do not

On your phone, check that Android Vision phone control is enabled in Accessibility settings. Start sharing with control approved and make sure control is not paused. Keep the phone unlocked. Text input depends on the target app and input method; some editors or language composition methods may behave differently.

Playback feels delayed or uneven

The app uses a small buffer to smooth audio and video, so a little delay after input is expected. Keep the devices near a reliable Wi-Fi access point and reduce other heavy network traffic. Performance varies by device, network, and source app.

Stop sharing or remove a device

Use Stop on the phone’s sharing notification, Disconnect on its dashboard, or Disconnect on the headset. You can pause control while continuing to view. Forget removes a saved peer from that device’s paired list; forget on both sides when you want both saved entries removed.

Optional diagnostics

Sharing works without analytics or crash reports. On Android, open Settings → Help improve Android Vision → Choose what to share to change the two separate choices. On Vision Pro, open Privacy & diagnostics to change crash reporting; the viewer does not include usage analytics. Both choices start off. Crash reports from an earlier run may be submitted when you next launch the app with crash reporting enabled. Turning this off stops future submissions but cannot recall reports already submitted for sending. See the privacy policy for the data involved.
