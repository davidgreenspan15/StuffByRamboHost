Refund Policy

Version: gesture-flow-refunds-2026-10-26-v1
Effective: October 26, 2026

We want compatibility and purchase terms to be clear before payment. Gesture Flow includes a one-time 14-day no-card trial, and paid v3 purchases also receive the refund protection below.

14-day voluntary refund window

You may request a full refund within 14 calendar days after the confirmed purchase date. Submit the request while signed in to the license portal. The server—not the browser—checks the purchase, payment, prior refund, and request date.

We may ask for enough information to locate the order, understand a technical problem, or protect against fraud. Never send a full card number, password, or one-time sign-in link.

After 14 days

After the voluntary window, refunds are generally not offered for a change of mind. We will still address duplicate or erroneous charges, a material failure to match the published description that we cannot reasonably correct, and any refund, cancellation, warranty, or other remedy required by applicable law.

Compatibility

Before paying, confirm that the Mac is Apple silicon, runs macOS 15 or later, has a working camera, and permits Camera and Accessibility access. Work-managed Macs can block installation or permissions. A mistaken compatibility purchase can be submitted within the 14-day window.

How a refund affects the license

A completed refund revokes the associated v3 entitlement and its active Mac. A Mac that is offline can retain cached access only until its next license validation, no later than 30 days after its last successful validation. You must stop using and remove the software when the license is revoked.

Processing and original payment method

Approved refunds are sent to the original payment method. The payment processor and your bank control when the credit appears; this commonly takes several business days and can take longer for some payment methods. Submitting a request does not itself confirm approval or completion.

Unrecognized charges and disputes

If you do not recognize a charge, contact support@stuffbyrambo.com promptly and use your card issuer’s security process when appropriate. Nothing in this policy restricts card-network dispute rights or mandatory legal remedies. A final reversal of the purchase payment revokes the related entitlement.

Abuse

We may refuse requests involving fraud, repeated trial or refund abuse, or a purchase already refunded or finally reversed, except where applicable law requires a remedy.

Contact

Use the license portal for a purchase-linked request. If portal access is unavailable, email support@stuffbyrambo.com.
