Compatibility and Permissions

Version: gesture-flow-compatibility-2026-10-26-v1
Effective: October 26, 2026

Confirm every item on this page before purchasing Gesture Flow v3. Downloading and trying the app before purchase is strongly recommended.

Supported Mac

  • Processor: Apple silicon Mac (M-series).
  • Operating system: macOS 15 or later.
  • Camera: a functioning built-in or compatible external camera positioned so your hands are visible.
  • Internet: required for sign-in, activation, updates, purchase fulfillment, and periodic license checks.

Intel Macs, Windows, Linux, iPhone, and iPad are not supported by this public lane. Virtual machines and remote/virtual camera configurations are not supported unless explicitly listed in a future update.

Required macOS permissions

Camera

Gesture Flow needs Camera access to process hand movements on the Mac. If access is denied, gesture recognition cannot work. Manage access in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera.

Accessibility

Gesture Flow needs Accessibility access to carry out supported system controls. If access is denied, the app may recognize a gesture but cannot reliably perform the requested control. Manage access in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility.

The current public lane processes camera frames on the Mac; the storefront and license service do not receive or store those frames. See the Privacy Policy.

Managed and restricted Macs

An employer, school, parental-control tool, security product, or mobile-device-management profile can block outside-app installation, Camera access, Accessibility access, or outbound connections. Ask the administrator before purchase. An administrator’s policy can make an otherwise compatible Mac unusable with Gesture Flow.

Trial, purchase, and devices

  • Eligible accounts receive one 14-day trial when they explicitly approve a device code.
  • No card is required, and the trial does not automatically become a paid plan.
  • A paid v3 entitlement supports one active Mac you own or control. The 14-day trial supports up to two active Macs.
  • Use the license portal to approve a Mac or deactivate an old one.

Offline use and revocation timing

After a successful license check, Gesture Flow can operate offline for up to 30 days. It must reconnect before that period ends. A device deactivation, completed refund, final payment reversal, or other valid revocation takes effect on the Mac at its next check and no later than the end of its cached 30-day offline window.

Installation and macOS security

macOS may show normal security and permission prompts during first launch. Only use the official download linked from stuffbyrambo.com or the project’s official GitHub release. Do not bypass a warning that the download is damaged, altered, or from an unidentified source; contact support instead.

What the v3 purchase includes

The one-time purchase licenses the Gesture Flow v3 major version. v3 updates may be provided while v3 is supported. A future major version, optional cloud feature, or subscription may be offered separately; the existing v3 license remains a v3 license and is not silently converted to recurring billing.

Compatibility help

If you are unsure whether a Mac qualifies, email support@stuffbyrambo.com before purchasing. Purchases also have the published 14-day refund window, subject to mandatory rights.
