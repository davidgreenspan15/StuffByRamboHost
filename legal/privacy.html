Privacy Policy

Version: gesture-flow-privacy-2026-10-26-v1
Effective: October 26, 2026

This policy explains how STUFF BY RAMBO LLC, based in Teaneck, New Jersey, USA (“we,” “us”), handles information for the Gesture Flow public Python / MediaPipe lane, storefront, trial, checkout integration, and license portal.

Camera and Accessibility processing

The public Python / MediaPipe lane processes camera frames on your Mac to recognize supported gestures. The storefront and license service do not receive or store those camera frames. Camera access is not used to identify you.

Gesture Flow uses macOS Accessibility permission to carry out the system controls you invoke. The license service does not receive screen content, keystrokes, or Accessibility data. If a future feature changes these practices, we will update this policy and provide notice before that collection begins.

Information we collect

  • Account and contact data: email address, sign-in request timestamps, and support communications.
  • Purchase data: Stripe customer, Checkout, payment, refund, and dispute identifiers; product/version; transaction status; price, currency, tax, and policy acceptance records. We do not receive or store full card numbers.
  • License data: entitlement and trial status, activation/deactivation history, an app-generated device identifier, a device label, app/macOS version, and license-check timestamps. We do not require a Mac serial number.
  • Security and operations data: limited request metadata such as a keyed, pseudonymous client-IP hash, coarse country code, time, endpoint, response status, and security/rate-limit events. Our application records do not retain the raw client IP address.
  • Refund and support data: reason category and details you choose to submit.

How we use information

  • Send one-time sign-in links and purchase activation codes, and secure the account.
  • Provide the one-time trial, fulfill purchases, and enforce one active Mac per paid license and up to two Macs during the trial.
  • Validate licenses, provide up to 30 days of offline use, and apply refunds or revocations.
  • Prevent fraud, repeated trial abuse, unauthorized access, and payment misuse.
  • Respond to support, compatibility, refund, and dispute requests.
  • Meet tax, accounting, legal, and security obligations.

Cookies and site technology

The license portal uses a necessary secure session cookie after you explicitly continue from a one-time email link. The intended production configuration is Secure, HttpOnly, and appropriately SameSite-restricted. The portal does not store its access token in browser local storage. These storefront, portal, and legal pages load no advertising or third-party runtime JavaScript.

Service providers and disclosures

We disclose only the information reasonably needed for these providers to perform their roles:

  • Stripe: hosted payment collection, receipts, refunds, fraud controls, disputes, and payment/tax services selected for the transaction. Stripe independently processes ordinary network and device information, including IP addresses, at hosted checkout.
  • Cloudflare: license API delivery, security, and rate limiting. Cloudflare processes ordinary network information, including IP addresses, to deliver and protect requests; our application stores only the pseudonymous hash and coarse country described above.
  • Supabase: authentication and storage for account, entitlement, device, and audit data.
  • Resend: delivery of account sign-in links and purchase activation codes. Email delivery records may contain the recipient address and message content, including an activation code.
  • GitHub: website and downloadable release hosting. GitHub independently processes ordinary network information, including IP addresses, when you visit or download.
  • Professional and legal recipients: accountants, counsel, regulators, courts, or law enforcement when reasonably necessary or legally required.

We do not sell personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use it for targeted advertising.

Retention

Account, entitlement, device, and purchase-linkage records are kept while needed to provide the license and resolve ownership, refund, fraud, or support questions. Financial and acceptance records are retained as required for tax, accounting, disputes, and legal compliance. Expired one-time links and detailed security logs are retained only as long as reasonably needed for authentication and security. We then delete or de-identify information unless continued retention is required by law.

Security

We use measures designed for the sensitivity of the data, including encrypted transport, restricted administrative access, row-level authorization, short-lived sign-in links, secure cookies, request rate limits, and server-side payment verification. No system can guarantee absolute security. Contact us promptly if you suspect misuse.

Your choices and requests

You can view devices and deactivate them through the license portal. You may request access, correction, deletion, or a copy of applicable personal information by emailing us. We may verify the request and retain records that must be kept for financial, fraud, security, or legal reasons. Depending on where you live, you may have additional non-waivable rights or an appeal process.

International use

Availability can be limited by checkout region. If you use the service outside the United States, information may be processed in the United States or other locations used by our providers, subject to their safeguards and applicable law.

Children

Gesture Flow and its direct-sale service are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from a child under 13. Contact us if you believe this occurred.

Changes

We may update this policy. The version and effective date appear above. Material changes will be communicated through the site, app, portal, or account email as appropriate.

Contact

Privacy questions and requests: support@stuffbyrambo.com.
