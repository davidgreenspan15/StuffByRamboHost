Red Tees Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 26, 2026

Red Tees (“we,” “us,” “the app”) helps Bergen County Golf members watch and book tee times. This policy describes what we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Who we are

Red Tees is operated by David (“Rami”) Greenspan in Teaneck, New Jersey. Contact: ramigreenspan@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you create an account

  • Email address — used to sign you in.
  • Name — used to greet you in the app.
  • Password — stored hashed with bcrypt. We never see it in plaintext after account creation.

When you link your Bergen County Golf account

  • Bergen County username and password — encrypted at rest with AES before being stored. We use them only to sign in to bergencountygolf.cps.golf on your behalf to search and book tee times.

When you use the app

  • Tee-time tickets and reservations you create.
  • Push notification tokens, if you enable notifications.
  • Anonymous Firebase Analytics data: app version, platform, locale, timezone, screen views, and an anonymous ID. You can disable analytics in the app’s privacy prompt.

We do not collect location, contacts, photos, or other phone data.

How we use it

  • Authenticate your sign-in.
  • Save tee-time preferences and search tickets.
  • Sign in to Bergen County Golf as you to search, book, or cancel reservations.
  • Send push notifications about booking results.
  • Use aggregate, anonymous analytics to improve the app.

How we share it

We do not sell or rent your data. We share data only with:

  • Bergen County Golf — your golf account credentials are sent to their site so we can act on your behalf.
  • Heroku, Inc. — backend hosting in the United States.
  • Google Firebase and Apple App Store / Google Play — delivery, analytics, and crash reporting.
  • Twilio SendGrid — booking-related email.

We may also share information if compelled by law.

How we protect it

  • Network traffic uses HTTPS/TLS.
  • Bergen County passwords are encrypted with AES-256 before storage.
  • App passwords are hashed with bcrypt and are not reversible.
  • Database access is restricted to authorized administrators.

How long we keep it

We keep account data while the account is active. When you delete your account from Profile → Delete account, we mark it deleted and disable access. Backups roll off within 30 days.

Your rights

  • Edit your name and password from Profile.
  • Delete your account from Profile → Delete account.
  • Request a copy of your data by emailing ramigreenspan@gmail.com.
  • Disable analytics from the in-app analytics consent prompt.

Children

Red Tees is intended for adults and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has used the app.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy. Material changes will be communicated through the app or account email. Questions: ramigreenspan@gmail.com.
